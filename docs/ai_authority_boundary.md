# AI Authority Boundary

This document explicitly defines the boundaries between Generative AI (stochastic) and Core Commerce (deterministic) within MerchantOS. By isolating these domains, we guarantee that the platform remains financially secure even under adversarial conditions (e.g., prompt injection, model hallucinations).

## 1. Legitimate Scope of AI (Allowed Actions)
The AI Agent operates strictly as an advisor, router, and explainer. Its authority is limited to:
- **Intent Extraction:** Parsing natural language to determine customer needs (e.g., budget constraints, desired categories).
- **Ranking & Recommendation:** Sorting a pre-filtered, server-validated list of products based on semantic relevance to the customer's intent.
- **Trade-off Explanation:** Generating plain-language comparisons based *only* on verified product metadata.
- **Campaign Hypothesis Generation:** Analyzing aggregated merchant telemetry to propose logical marketing segments and predicted uplift ranges.
- **Offer Contextualization:** Producing concise, helpful text explaining why a specific pre-computed offer is being proposed to the customer.

## 2. Deterministic Systems Authority (AI Exclusions)
The AI Agent **MUST NEVER** have authority over the following. These are strictly controlled by server-side deterministic logic and database constraints:
- **Pricing & Arithmetic:** The AI cannot set item prices, apply custom discounts, calculate tax/delivery, or sum totals. All financial arithmetic runs on the server.
- **Inventory & Eligibility:** The AI cannot verify stock levels, claim inventory, determine if a user qualifies for a campaign, or assert merchant ownership.
- **Policy Enforcement:** Budget limits, minimum margin floors, and maximum discount ceilings are enforced deterministically. AI proposals exceeding these limits are silently dropped or clamped.
- **Commercial State Mutations:** The AI cannot add items to the cart, accept an offer, create a payment order, capture funds, or initiate a refund. All mutations require an explicit cryptographic client-to-server action independent of the LLM generation.

## 3. Threat Model & Untrusted Inputs
- **User Input:** Assumed malicious. Parsed for intent, but never evaluated as a command that mutates financial state.
- **Catalog Metadata (Descriptions, Names):** Assumed untrusted. Can contain adversarial text. Evaluated safely and stripped of instructional formatting before ranking.
- **Model Output (JSON/Text):** Assumed unreliable. Tool arguments generated by the model (e.g., `discountPercentage`) are treated as *requests*, not facts. They are immediately validated against strict Zod schemas and clamped by deterministic merchant policies.
